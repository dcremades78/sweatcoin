01 · What we found

What we saw when we looked at Sweatcoin on social.

Before writing anything, we looked at how people talk about Sweatcoin and step-reward apps today. Four things stood out.

A huge product

190M+ users. The creator layer is still thin.

Sweatcoin is reported to have 190M+ registered users and to rank #1 in Health & Fitness in 58 countries. Yet much of what shows up under #sweatcoin is user-made tutorials and "how to cash out" videos, not briefed creators.

One question

Everyone asks: "does it actually pay?"

Reviews and comments keep coming back to the same doubt, and some videos promise earnings that aren't realistic. A creator who answers it honestly, with a real redemption on camera, earns trust no ad can buy.

People search for it

TikTok is a search engine for this.

TikTok already has search pages for "walking app to get paid legit" and "walk pay app reviews". Videos made for those searches keep bringing installs long after they're posted. It's the approach we designed for Flo's US rollout.

The format works

Step-reward apps already pay creators for it.

"I've been walking for money, here's how much" with a referral code is a common format in the UK and the US. Sweatcoin is the name people know. It should own that format with native creators.

04 · A wildcard worth testing

Three creator types nobody expects from a fitness app.

Next to the shortlist, we'd keep a small part of the first wave for three creator types outside the usual fitness crowd. A few creators each, measured like the rest. If one works, it becomes a channel of its own.

Wildcard 1

Dog walkers and dog accounts

People with dogs walk twice a day, rain or shine. They're the most consistent walkers Sweatcoin could have. Accounts that "talk" in the dog's voice travel far and are brand-safe in the US and the UK.

"POV: I'm the reason my human gets paid to walk."
#dogsoftiktok · #dogwalk · #dogmom
Wildcard 2

Jobs with thousands of steps

Nurses, mail carriers, servers and warehouse staff can walk 15,000 steps or more in a single shift. They're the most believable answer to "does it actually pay?", with real numbers. Big, loyal communities that nobody expects from a fitness app.

"I walk 20,000 steps a shift. Here's what Sweatcoin gave me this month."
#nurselife · #mailcarrier · #serverlife
Wildcard 3

Animated characters

Illustrators with a recurring character who walks and earns through a weekly series. Telling it as a story keeps it far from "get rich walking" hype. It's a format that worked very well for us with Flo.

A week in the life of one character, Monday to Sunday, step counter included.
#animation · #animatedseries · #illustration
How we'd test it: two or three creators per wildcard inside the first wave, each with their own link, so you can compare cost per install against the main shortlist. We'd scout these creators after our call.

How a first wave would work.

Start small, measure installs per creator and keep only what works. We'd agree budget and timing together after a call, once we know what matters most to you.
